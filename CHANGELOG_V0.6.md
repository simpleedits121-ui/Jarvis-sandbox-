# JARVIS Character Forge v0.6

## Geometry upgrade
- Smooth indexed meshes with averaged vertex normals instead of duplicating every triangle vertex with flat normals.
- 36 named mesh parts in the generated character.
- Layered eye whites, iris, pupils and highlights.
- Curved tapered hair locks, layered fringe and back-hair sections.
- Better face silhouette, ears, nose, mouth, hands/fingers, puff sleeves, layered skirt and trim.
- 15 PBR palette materials.
- GLB, OBJ, MTL and JSON generation report.

## Still pending
- UV unwrapping and painted texture maps.
- Production-quality facial topology and garment simulation/folds.
- Humanoid skeleton, skin weights, facial rig and animation.
- Neural image understanding and automatic reference reconstruction.

This release is an offline procedural quality step, not a claim of parity with commercial image-to-3D services.
